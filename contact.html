Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, we are pet-friendly! Please inquire when booking so we can arrange the right accommodation for you and your furry companion. An additional pet fee may apply. We have an enclosed outdoor dog park on site.

We are at 4586 US Route 5, Barnet, Vermont 05050 — in the historic village of McIndoe Falls, along the Connecticut River. We are just 2.5 miles from I-91 Exit 18 (Barnet). GPS works well; look for us on the left heading south on US Route 5 from the highway exit.

Standard check-in is between 3:00 PM and 7:00 PM. Check-out is by 11:00 AM. If you expect to arrive after 7:00 PM, please call ahead so we can make arrangements. We are flexible and want your arrival to go smoothly.

Yes — extended stay discounts are available, particularly for our annex apartments which have full kitchens. Contact Gloria and Staff directly to discuss weekly rates and multi-night arrangements that work for your schedule.

Cancellations 14+ days before arrival receive a full refund. Cancellations 7–14 days before arrival receive a 50% refund. Cancellations within 7 days are non-refundable. If you have extenuating circumstances, contact Gloria and Staff — they are human beings and will do their best to work with you.

Yes to both — free WiFi is available throughout the inn, and free on-site parking is included. Note that cell service in the Northeast Kingdom can be spotty (Verizon tends to work best north of Brattleboro), so WiFi is especially handy during your stay.
